About April

Coach. Trainer.
Runner.

April Hargis is a Certified Personal Trainer (CPT) and Registered Yoga Teacher (RYT) who specializes in helping everyday runners achieve goals they never thought possible.

She combines evidence-based run coaching with yoga-informed mobility and strength training | a whole-body approach that builds runners who are fast, resilient, and injury-resistant.

April is passionate about making running accessible to everyone, especially those who feel intimidated or sidelined by their own bodies. Her coaching style is encouraging, data-informed, and always human-first.
